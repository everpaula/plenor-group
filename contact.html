Your first email

What to include.

Four lines, no more. The shorter the brief, the faster the first conversation gets to the operating question.

  • Company name and ARR band. Range is fine.
  • Operating problem in one sentence. What is broken or what is missing.
  • Timeline pressure. Board cycle, hiring window, capital event, or other.
  • How you found Plenor. Referral name, content piece, or search.
What happens next

Three steps to a yes-or-no decision.

  1. 15-minute intro. A direct read on whether Plenor is the right fit for the problem. Honest if the answer is no.
  2. 60-minute scoping. If the fit holds, a deeper conversation with the operating leader who will own the engagement. Scoped questions, no slide deck.
  3. Written proposal in 7 days. Scope, phases, deliverables, timeline, pricing structure. One document. Yes-or-no decision on the client side.
