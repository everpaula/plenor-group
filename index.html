About

Built by an operator. For operators.

Plenor Group operates at the meeting point of operator math and executive decisions. Founded by Everton Paula, Lean Six Sigma Master Black Belt with 15+ years building operations functions from zero.

Most recent role: Head of Business Operations at a global ride-hailing platform, $40M P&L across 8 countries. Before that: scaling marketplace fulfillment across multiple regions, building courier operations across 9 markets at hypergrowth, and redesigning customer operations at a major insurance incumbent.

The thesis is simple. Most ops consulting sells slides. We ship the operating engine that holds up under load, then step away.
