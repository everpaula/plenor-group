Why Plenor exists

The gap between strategy and execution is a market gap.

The gap between strategy and execution is a market gap, not a personal one. MBB firms produce strategy decks at high quality and disband at presentation. Executive search firms produce full-time hires at high quality and take nine months to do it. Fractional executive marketplaces produce staffing matches at variable quality and treat the engagement as a placement, not an operating outcome.

None of the three is structured to install an operating engine in 90 to 180 days, with one accountable operator, with written deliverables, and with a named handoff to an internal owner at exit. Plenor is. The firm exists because that work is the difference between a strategy that ships and a strategy that sits, and somebody has to do it as a job, not as a side conversation.

How we work

One operator. Written scope. Named exit.

One accountable operator owns every engagement. No analyst pyramid, no layered staffing model, no junior team doing the work while a partner manages the relationship. The operator who scopes the engagement is the operator who runs the engagement.

Every engagement has a written scope, a written cadence, a written exit shape, and named deliverables at named milestones. Weekly status is written, not spoken. Final artifacts are running systems with documented playbooks, not slide decks. The default outcome is that Plenor leaves on the named date, the internal team owns the operating engine, and the operating metrics hold for 60 days post-handoff without Plenor in the room.
